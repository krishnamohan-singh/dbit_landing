ABOUT VIDYASHILP UNIVERSITY WHO WE REALLY ARE

Vidyashilp University, Bengaluru's forward-thinking liberal education institution, is dedicated to shaping the leaders of tomorrow. Its interdisciplinary curriculum allows students to seamlessly blend Data Science with fields like Design, Business, Psychology, and Economics, creating a personalised learning journey that sparks creativity and critical thinking.

With a 1:10 faculty-student ratio, state-of-the-art labs, and mentors drawn from industry and academia, every student receives individual attention and real-world exposure. Here, young minds evolve into confident, adaptable professionals equipped to solve complex challenges and drive meaningful change in an ever-evolving world.
